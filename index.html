Our Story

About
Tessera

Tessera Compassion Foundation began not with a plan, but with a person.

While in high school, founder Aiden Kim met an unhoused elderly woman. When he offered her money, she gently declined and shared that what she truly needed was medication for herself and her son.

That encounter changed the way he understood compassion. It eventually led him to study pharmacy and dedicate himself to serving underserved communities through outreach in areas such as Stockton, Compton, and Skid Row.

Believing in the power of storytelling, Aiden invested his personal savings to travel to Korea, where he began creating simple videos that gave moments of rest, joy, and appreciation to elderly people working hard but living in poverty. What started as a small act of compassion gradually grew into a global movement.

Today, Tessera Compassion Foundation is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to restoring dignity, fostering sustainable hope, and serving vulnerable communities through compassion, storytelling, and action, one story, one person, one tessera at a time.

We don't let compassion stay only in our hearts. We connect people through stories, and we create real change through action.

The Meaning

What is
a Tessera?

"Tessera" is derived from the Greek word for four and also refers to a small piece of a mosaic, a single tile whose purpose is revealed only when placed alongside others.

At Tessera Compassion Foundation, the number four reflects our commitment to the quartet of the vulnerable: low income families, orphans, widows, and immigrants and refugees.

Just as every tessera is essential to completing a mosaic, we believe every person has inherent dignity, and every act of compassion, no matter how small, helps restore dignity and create sustainable hope.
